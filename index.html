Quick refresher
  • Bearings are measured clockwise from North and written as 3‑figure angles, e.g. \(045^\circ\), \(120^\circ\), \(290^\circ\).
  • You can resolve distances into north–south and east–west components, then use Pythagoras and \(\tan\theta\) to find distances and bearings.
